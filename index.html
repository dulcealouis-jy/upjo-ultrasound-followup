Clinical association in the study cohort: recurrent PUJO requiring reoperation occurred in 9/77 (11.7%) renal units with at least one APD above the upper reference curve and in 4/487 (0.8%) that remained within the curve (risk ratio 14.23, 95% CI 4.49–45.08; Fisher exact P<0.001).
The reference values describe postoperative renal pelvic dilatation in the study cohort. Ultrasound findings should be interpreted with symptoms, urinary tract infection, renal parenchymal appearance and renal functional assessment.
How the assessment is calculated

The expected median and upper reference value are interpolated from baseline- and time-specific curves derived after primary pyeloplasty for isolated PUJO. A previous postoperative APD can be entered to show whether dilatation is decreasing, stable or increasing. Persistent dilatation above the upper reference value, interval increase or associated clinical concern should prompt clinical review and consideration of functional imaging.
